# ACCOUNTANT brief — rulebook verified on TRAIN golden (scripts tmp/acc/a1-a6.py, cache pq/m2.parquet with per-item ledger k=#prior payments, rem_after)
"1-item"=line with single golden item; short=total-cash (cents, same ccy).

## A. RULEBOOK
R1 OVERPAYMENT: 1-item ARI receipt, same ccy, cash>open -> amount=open, OVERPAYMENT. 2,838/2,838, all k=0. Multi-item lines never have cash>sum golden (0). Excess median 1%.
R2 SHORT_DISCOUNT: terms with discount (D2N30,D2N30US,D1N60,D3N45); short==round_half_up(total*pct/100) (exact 98.9%, ±1c 100%); BASE=TOTAL (net 46.8%); booking_date-issue_date <= discount_days+3. In-window discount-shaped 1-item: 38,854 all SD; outside: 3,232 all PARTIAL (ar_discount_late, amount=cash); 0 crossover. No 2% short without discount terms. Multi-invoice: per row, cash==Σ(total-disc on SD rows)+Σother-ΣCN in 100% of 4,829 lines; rows on a line can mix in/out of window. Use booking_date, not value_date.
R3 SHORT_WITHHOLDING: short==rate*TOTAL (exact ~97%, ±1c 100%). BR01/MA01 {1.5%,10%}; MX01 {1.25%,10%}; ES01 10% only for customers country MA. Net base fails. Rate is FIXED PER CUSTOMER (516 customers, 0 with two rates); a withholding customer withholds on ~90% of full payments. Arithmetic-only precision on BR/MA/MX first payments 98.8% (8,630 vs 105 PARTIAL). Trap: ES01 302 PARTIAL lines with 10% short (retention). Multi-invoice (15%): flag every row (96%).
R4 SHORT_BANK_CHARGES: 1-item ARI receipt; customers.country != entities.country (item's entity); 0<short<=2% of total (and <=~60EUR); not discount-shaped. Precision 98.7% (1,083 SBC vs 14 PARTIAL after excluding discount shape). NOT a SEPA list: pairs CA→US 497, NZ→AU 211, MA→ES 70, GB→ES 49, DZ→ES 49, PL→DE 46, CH→IT 39, MT→IT 25, CH→FR 20... (PL, MT flagged). Always exactly one item.
R5 PARTIAL generic: 1-item same-ccy receipt cash<open and not R2-R4 -> amount=cash. k=0: 36,006 PARTIAL vs 1,626 none (645 card orders gross vs net; rest instalment/retention ordering). k>0 leaving balance: 11,658 PARTIAL vs 812 none.
R6 closing remainder: cash==current open balance (<total) -> none, amount=open balance (ar_partial 13,477, ar_instalment 8,774, ar_dispute_settlement 7,026, ar_retention_release 3,920).
R7 INSTALMENTS decided by AMOUNT not order: piece=floor(total/n), last=total-(n-1)*piece. Row with amount==last -> none 6,329/6,329; others PARTIAL 15,681 vs 138 none — even if "last" is booked before an ordinary piece (ARI-2021-0037713: 53,183c none 2022-04-12, then 53,178c PARTIAL rem_after=0). "closes item -> none" wrong in 726 cases. n in 2..6 (3 mode). Gap median 32d p90 63. Text often identical across pieces; RIBA "PRESTACAO 2/2". n only in PDF ("3 vencimientos"); otherwise infer from total/cash ≈ integer 2..6.
R8 RETENTION: first payment 0.95 or 0.90 of total -> PARTIAL; release = remaining 5% (2,725) / 10% (1,265) -> none, median 339d later (p10 267, p90 393, max 439). 40% never released in TRAIN. 20 releases before main payment. Text "RET. GARANTIA FV…", "RETENTION RELEASE 205804", plain ACH.
R9 SHORT_DISPUTE from advice reason (COOP MKT, PRICE DIFF, LOGISTIC FEE, SHORT DELIVERY, PENAL. RETRASO, DAMAGED GOODS); exactly one row per line; amount=paid (0.70-0.97). 1-item lines: only 2 cases -> without advice, short is PARTIAL. Settlement: remainder fixed 0.03/0.05/0.10/0.20/0.30 of total, median 55d later (p10 27, max 92), none, amount=remaining open; text "SETTLEMENT <doc>" (97% full doc). Only 33% of disputes ever settled.
R10 a cuenta (ar_partial): 2 pieces: PARTIAL then remainder none (13,360/13,886). Gap median 45d max 78; first ratio ~U(0.3,0.75). Remainder text "RESTANTE" or doc again.
R11 ADVANCE deduction: always PARTIAL (2,003/2,003), ratio 0.5-0.7; rest NEVER paid (proforma covers it).
R12 LATE DISCOUNT: PARTIAL amount=cash=98%; residual 2% paid later only 2.4% (inside ar_n_to_m sweeps).
R13 DUPLICATE_PAYMENT: item already closed, paid again; amount=cash=total (AR 100%); always 1 item. AR: first payment ar_ref_noise 2,820 / ar_1to1 1,580; gap median 7d (p10 2, max 15); same account 94.6%; identical text 58%. AP (debit): duplicate text identical to first, no e2e; gap median 28d (p10 14, max 50); 467/484 full amount, 17 duplicate an ap_partial piece. Detect: referenced API already closed, same amount, same text, <=50d.
R14 REFUND OF DUPLICATE (category): CUSTOMER_REFUND (debit): 2,562/2,611 have earlier AR DUPLICATE of exactly |amount| same account within 47d (median 27), unique 2,559. Texts "SEPA-UEBERWEISUNG AN <party>", "REFUND DUPLICATE PAYMENT <doc>", "DEVOLUCION PAGO DUPLICADO". SUPPLIER_REFUND (credit): 479/483 have earlier AP duplicate same amount within 50d (median 32); texts "DEVOLUCION PAGO DUPLICADO", "REEMBOLSO INV…". Otherwise same keywords settle an open APC (ap_supplier_refund 17,795). Discriminator = earlier AP duplicate of same amount exists.
R15 RETURNED (amount = original allocation re-opened = item total):
 - DD return: mandate in text (MREF+ / MANDATO / MANDATE VTM-xx-…).
 - Cheque: "RETURNED ITEM CHK nnnnnnn", "CHEQUE IMPAYE nnnnnnn"; cheque number found in cheque_deposits 535/538; original always ar_cheque_deposit; stub==doc 371; use golden item of that deposit row.
 - Card chargeback: "CHARGEBACK ****dddd MID nnn"; last4+MID+gross==|cash| unique order 212/289 (73%); rest need wider settlement-file search (prev month/other file).
 - AP returned transfer (credit): "DEVOLUCION TRANSFERENCIA AC04 | <supplier>", "RETURN AC04 <supplier> PR0100…-0054"; e2e of payment run; ALL items of original transfer incl. APC (64 rows positive); cash net of CN.
 - After: re-presentation (DD batch re_presentation=Y) or transfer "PAGO RECIBO <suffix>" (ar_dd_represented_transfer) clears RETURNED item, none; median 18d (max 33); golden = re-opened item 7,488/7,488; FIFO would pick it only 7% -> look up "re-opened by return" first.
R16 UNIDENTIFIED: 23% (7,461/34,653) unidentified receipts equal an open ARI amount same entity+ccy; only 229 (0.7%) also share payer first-name token. Never amount alone; name+exact amount safe (ar_ref_noise unique in party 99.97%).
R17 CREDIT NOTES: ARC 55,798 returns/corrections + 1,132 rappels (related_document null). related invoice on the same line only 50% -> hint not selector. Select subset with invoices-notes==cash (100% of 47,187 no-exception lines). ar_credit_note_paid_out (325): debit settles ARC alone.
R18 N-TO-M (ar_n_to_m 13,904 lines): 6,824 include carried-over remainder (k>0); 6,937 have exactly one PARTIAL row; never >1 PARTIAL per line; partial item is latest-due only 56% -> not FIFO waterfall; use doc list, leftover goes to the single invoice not fully covered.
R19 LIVE CARRY-OVER: open_items_at_start 30,827 ARI + 1,264 API with open<total (0 CN). open/total ratio 0.02 (9,975 late-discount residue, rarely paid), 0.05 (4,386), 0.10 (3,535) (retention/dispute), 0.01/0.03/0.2/0.3 (dispute). Payment of these = retention release / dispute settlement: amount=open_amount, none. Ledger tests start from open_amount; discount/withholding arithmetic uses TOTAL (but residues never discounted, k>0).

## B. EXCEPTION DECISION TABLE (per item row, in order)
1 return/chargeback/cheque return/rejected AP transfer -> RETURNED, item total (all items incl APC)
2 item already closed, same amount paid again (1 item) -> DUPLICATE_PAYMENT, cash
3 cross-ccy & invoice row fully cleared -> FX_DIFFERENCE (replaces none/SD/SW/SBC), open in item ccy; 3b cross-ccy CN row -> none
4 netted CN -> none, full positive
5 advice deduction with dispute reason (one row per line) -> SHORT_DISPUTE (beats FX/PARTIAL), paid part
6 instalment != plan last piece; first retention payment 0.90/0.95; advance-deduction invoice; late discount -> PARTIAL (beats FX), cash
7 short==pct*total & booking<=issue+disc_days+3 -> SHORT_DISCOUNT, total
8 short==rate*total BR/MA/MX (or ES01+MA customer) -> SHORT_WITHHOLDING, total
9 1 item, payer country != entity country, 0<short<=2%/60EUR -> SHORT_BANK_CHARGES, total
10 1 item cash>open -> OVERPAYMENT, open
11 cash==current open (last instalment by amount, retention release, dispute settlement, re-presented DD, carry-over remainder) -> none, open
12 cash<open otherwise -> PARTIAL, cash
- receipt w/o item evidence -> category UNIDENTIFIED + UNIDENTIFIED

## C. TRAPS
1 "closes item -> none" fails for instalments/retentions out of order (726+20). 2 10% short = retention in ES01 unless customer MA; BR/MA/MX have 105 PARTIAL withholding-shaped. 3 non-SEPA = country mismatch (PL, MT flagged). 4 discount base TOTAL, half-up, booking_date, +3 inclusive; boundary perfectly clean. 5 withholding base TOTAL. 6 never OVERPAYMENT/SBC/DUP on multi-item; never >1 SHORT_DISPUTE or >1 PARTIAL per n_to_m line. 7 card orders cleared at GROSS no exception (645 1-order lines look PARTIAL). 8 residues mostly never paid (advance 0%, late discount 2.4%, retention 60% unreleased, dispute 33% settled); LIVE carries ~31k — don't force receipts on them. 9 customer refund of duplicate = category, pairs with earlier DUP same amount same account <=47d; supplier "REEMBOLSO/PAGO DOBLE" = SUPPLIER_REFUND only if we double-paid, else settles APC. 10 AP duplicates identical text, no e2e — doc resolver re-matches them; must flag because API closed. 11 returned AP transfers include netted APC rows (positive, RETURNED); cash != Σ golden. 12 transfer after DD return goes to RETURNED item (FIFO right 7%). 13 unidentified matches open invoice by amount 23% -> need name/ref. 14 related_document same line only 50%; rappels none. 15 chargebacks last4+MID only 73% in visible files; search adjacent months.
