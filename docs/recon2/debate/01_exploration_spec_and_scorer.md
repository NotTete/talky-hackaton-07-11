# Ground-truth contract (organiser sources only: docs/, vendor/bankrec_score.py, masters, schema.py)
V:n = vendor/bankrec_score.py line n. [TRAIN] = counted on data/train/golden_allocations.csv.gz.

## 1. Scoring as implemented
- score = round(100*(0.5*F1 + 0.3*acc + 0.2*macro),2) (V:28,V:325).
- Tolerance: _tol(g)=max(0.01,0.005*|g|)+1e-9; abs(amt-g)<=tol in FLOAT on golden (V:34,V:255). Golden <2.00 -> 0.01. NOTE repo schema.py tolerance max(1,round(0.005*cents)) can be 0.5c looser than scorer — trust scorer.
- Golden load V:59-99: every phase bank line seeded items={},cat="",exc="". Item rows -> items[item]=(amt,exc); category rows set cat/exc. amount_eur weight is server-only, not in score. Allowed exc excludes empty. Category list passed by caller (score_train reads masters/categories.csv).
- Allocation F1 (V:227-263,311-314): every accepted item row = submitted; TP iff item in golden for same line AND amount within tol (exception ignored). P=TP/submitted, R=TP/golden pairs. Category rows excluded.
- Txn accuracy (V:286-315): over ALL phase lines, unweighted. Item line correct iff no cat row, #rows == golden #items, every row TP AND exc==golden exc (empty==empty). Category line correct iff exactly 1 cat row with cat==golden and exc==golden. Missing lines = wrong.
- Exception macro-F1 (V:259-279,316-324): per ROW. Item row with non-empty exc: TP iff exc==golden exc for that item on that line (NO amount check); item not in golden line -> FP. Cat row with non-empty exc: TP iff golden line has no items and exc==golden exc (category not checked). Gold counts = golden rows with non-empty exc. Classes with gold count 0 skipped (FPs free). Empty never a class.
- Undocumented: F1 ignores exception; exception term ignores amount & category; category only scored via txn accuracy; cat/exc trimmed+upper-cased (V:209-210); header lowercased, 5 cols any order; amount regex ^-?\d+(\.\d+)?$; gzip by magic bytes; concatenated gzip ok; trailing NUL tolerated (V:115).

## 2. Format / validation (any error rejects whole file)
Header bank_txn_id,item_id,amount,category,exception. Item row: item_id, positive amount in ITEM currency, empty category. Category row: empty item_id+amount, one category.
Reject: empty file; bad gzip; >80MB decompress / >60MB; not UTF-8; NUL in first 4KB; >3M rows; missing/extra column; field count; empty/unknown bank_txn_id; unknown exception/category; item_id not ^(ARI|ARC|API|APC)-\d{4}-\d{7}$; category on item row; invalid amount; duplicate (line,item); mixing items+category on a line; amount on cat row; 2nd cat row.
NOT validated: item existence (unknown id = FP), missing lines.

## 3. Closed lists
Exceptions (POLICIES §3):
- (empty): paid as documented. Also last instalment, retention release, remainder after partial, dispute settlement, re-presented DD, netted credit notes.
- PARTIAL: item still has open balance after this line and no specific reason. "A CUENTA", instalments except last, first payment with retention, invoice with advance to deduct (PDF), late discount. amount=cash applied.
- SHORT_DISCOUNT: terms have discount, booking_date <= invoice date + discount_days + 3 grace, cash = total - pct*total (rounded to cent). Fully cleared; amount = full open.
- SHORT_BANK_CHARGES: non-SEPA cross-border, intermediary fee (SHA/BEN), shortfall <=60 EUR equiv AND <=2% of item. Above -> PARTIAL.
- SHORT_WITHHOLDING: some MX/BR/MA customers withhold 1.25%, 1.5% or 10% of invoice total. Fully cleared.
- SHORT_DISPUTE: deduction with reason (price, damaged, short delivery, late penalty, co-op, logistics fee...) usually only in remittance advice. Amount=paid; rest open; later payment of remainder = no exception.
- OVERPAYMENT: pays more than open amount of a single invoice. amount=open amount; excess no row.
- DUPLICATE_PAYMENT: already-settled item paid again; LATER line flagged; amount=paid again. Refund of duplicate = category CUSTOMER_REFUND/SUPPLIER_REFUND.
- RETURNED: DD return (R-code), returned cheque, card chargeback, our supplier transfer rejected. Every item of returned operation gets a row; amount=re-opened.
- UNIDENTIFIED: receipt with no open item -> one row category UNIDENTIFIED + exception UNIDENTIFIED. "Do not force a match: some unidentified receipts have exactly the amount of an unrelated open invoice."
- FX_DIFFERENCE: line currency != invoice currency; spread ~0.1-0.6% G10 (USD GBP CHF CAD AUD PLN), 0.4-1.5% emerging (MXN BRL CNY ZAR MAD); 3% FX tolerance. ALL fully cleared invoice rows get FX_DIFFERENCE (replacing empty/SHORT_DISCOUNT/SHORT_WITHHOLDING/SHORT_BANK_CHARGES). PARTIAL/SHORT_DISPUTE keep code; credit-note rows stay empty. amount=full open in invoice ccy.
Categories (code|desc|[TRAIN] sign): BANK_FEES debit; INTEREST_INCOME credit; INTEREST_EXPENSE debit; PAYROLL debit; SOCIAL_SECURITY debit; TAX_PAYMENT debit; TAX_REFUND credit; LOAN_DRAWDOWN credit; LOAN_REPAYMENT debit; LEASING debit; FX_DEAL both; CASH_POOL 50/50; INTERCOMPANY both; INTERNAL_TRANSFER 50/50; CUSTOMER_ADVANCE credit (proforma); CUSTOMER_REFUND debit; SUPPLIER_REFUND credit (refund of our duplicate, no credit note); CARD_EXPENSES debit; INSURANCE debit; BANK_REVERSAL 50/50 (error + "ANULACION OPERACION INDEBIDA"); UNIDENTIFIED credit.
[TRAIN] only non-empty exception on a category row: UNIDENTIFIED/UNIDENTIFIED (34,653).

## 4. Policy rules (numbered)
R1 one row per settled item (positive, item ccy, credit notes included) or exactly one category row. R2 one exception per row. R3 only ARI/ARC/API/APC are items (not proformas/advices/runs).
R4 normal: open amount. R5 netted credit note: full amount, positive, no exception. R6 SHORT_*/FX/OVERPAYMENT: full open. R7 PARTIAL/SHORT_DISPUTE: cash applied. R8 DUP: paid again. R9 RETURNED: re-opened. R10 tol max(0.01,0.5%).
R11 FX overrides empty/SD/SW/SBC on fully cleared invoice rows; PARTIAL & SHORT_DISPUTE beat FX; credit notes empty. R12 SHORT_DISPUTE beats PARTIAL. R13 late discount -> PARTIAL; charges >60EUR or >2% -> PARTIAL. R14 last instalment/retention release/remainder/dispute settlement/re-presented DD/netted CN -> empty. R15 later payment is the duplicate. R16 UNIDENTIFIED only when no open item; never amount alone. R17 discount test booking_date vs invoice date+days+3; cash=total-pct*total rounded. R18 WHT 1.25/1.5/10% MX BR MA. R19 bank charges non-SEPA <=60EUR & <=2%.
R20 BANK_FEES: maintenance, per-transfer, intl transfer fees (separate line same day), SEPA batch fees, return fees, card terminal fees. R21 card acquirer settlement clears orders at GROSS; acquirer fee contractual, no exception. R22 CASH_POOL both sides. R23 INTERNAL_TRANSFER same entity own accounts; INTERCOMPANY group entities; FX_DEAL both legs. R24 BANK_REVERSAL both. R25 proforma payment -> CUSTOMER_ADVANCE; later invoice ("a deducir anticipo") paid as PARTIAL. R26 refund customer duplicate -> CUSTOMER_REFUND; supplier refunds our duplicate -> SUPPLIER_REFUND; supplier refunding a credit note settles the APC.
R27 payer may be parent (group_id, parent_customer_id), confirming bank/factor ("CONFIRMING ... ORD. <customer>"), collection agency ("EXPTE"); allocate to invoices of the customer paid for. R28 ES00 pays supplier invoices of ES01/ES02/PT01 (payment factory). R29 DD batch clears all receipts; card settlement its orders; cheque deposit its cheques; aggregated/confirming debit all run docs; per-transfer debit the docs with that end_to_end_id.
R30 advices: PDF/CSV/XLSX/email txt, can arrive days late, customer voucher numbers or our number without prefix; one customer changes layout Sep 2025; bank text may only carry customer's payment ref. R31 refs noisy: no prefix/leading zeros, typos, transposed digits, truncation at 16/35/38, customer PO, old cancelled number, no ref (name/IBAN only); names truncated/trade names; renamed customers show current name.
R32 same amount no ref -> oldest open invoice (FIFO by invoice date); if text names a month -> that service month's invoice (invoiced start of following month; PDF names month). R33 "Anula y sustituye a X": payment citing X settles replacement; X and its cancelling CN never settled by bank. R34 instalments ("3 vencimientos"), 5-10% retention (released ~12 months later), advance to deduct appear only in invoice PDF. R35 return/price CNs carry related_document; rappel CNs quarterly offset or paid out per PDF (paid out = our outgoing transfer settles ARC); supplier CNs offset in next run or refunded per PDF. R36 returned DD re-presented later (re_presentation=Y) or paid by transfer. R37 PDF notices of new bank details; IT01 new collections account 2026-02-01, both valid.
